# Understanding and knowledge

*The operator's distinction (2026-10-08): a people that understands a thing but builds nothing with it has
**understanding**. A people that builds technology with it has **knowledge**.*

## The three ledgers

`xylarium/kernel/ledgers.py` reads all three for any people:

| Ledger | What it is | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **Understanding** | What a people has thought and studied without yet building with it | `knowledge.stocks` (lived learning and surplus thought, per domain); `intellect` crystals (the forms its minds think with); `learning` lessons (what its choices taught it) |
| **Knowledge** | Understanding it has built with | Capabilities its own understanding found and made work (`technology.season`); proofs its minds built (`intellect` ladders and certificates; `court` tasks closed) |
| **Unearned** | Capabilities held **without** understanding | Granted by its history's stage (`annals._grant` → `technology.grant`); dug up and not yet proven (`relics` "unproven") |

## The laws

1. **Living and thinking build understanding.** Every member of a people thinks as it lives, so `knowledge.lived` accrues
   with or without surplus. Its free hands add more (`knowledge.effort`). The annals carry both through a people's deep
   years in closed form, by the same laws the engine's quarters use. A people that studies nothing yet studies every
   domain alike.
2. **Understanding builds knowledge.** A capability is found where a people's understanding of its domain and its
   thought allow it, on a world whose physics lets it work (`technology.season`). Found that way, it is knowledge.
3. **Unearned is usable but not extended.** A capability granted by a people's stage, or dug from a relic, works. But
   nothing that builds on it (`technology.AFTER`) can be found until it is earned.
4. **Understanding earns the unearned.** Each year an unearned capability becomes knowledge with the same chance its
   finding would have: its domain's understanding, times the thought the people has. That is an `EARNED` event in its
   history. A relic's capability earned this way is proven.
5. **Inheritance carries all three.** A people that becomes another (genome), whose worlds pass to a ruler (roles), or
   that splits (relics) carries what it held, unearned as unearned.

## Owed

- **Stage turnings from what was built.** The annals still grant by stage, and those grants are now recorded as
  unearned. The next step is for a people's turnings to follow from the knowledge it built, rather than granting it.
- **Repair.** Technology held without understanding cannot be repaired. This needs a wear-and-repair law for works,
  which the prototype does not model yet.

## Why

The live universe showed the gap. The Okishsha, an ancient people, were handed fission, robotics and genetics by the
annals' stage grants while every domain of their understanding stood at 0. They were a cargo cult in the books. Under
these ledgers their capabilities read as unearned, and their lived understanding earns them.

The xylarium port carries the same split: its `knowledge.rs` stocks are understanding, and it adds a built ledger.
